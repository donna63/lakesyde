About Ruth Buabeng

Ruth Buabeng, PMHNP-BC, is a board-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with over 14 years of nursing experience caring for individuals across diverse healthcare settings. Her clinical background includes behavioral health, inpatient psychiatric care, long-term care, post-acute care, and home health.

Ruth earned her Master of Science in Nursing with a specialization in Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner from Eastern Kentucky University. Her approach to care is compassionate, patient-centered, and evidence-based.

"I believe in taking the time to listen to each patient, understand their individual needs, and work collaboratively to develop a treatment plan that supports their mental health and overall well-being."

Ruth Buabeng, PMHNP-BC
Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

Ruth is passionate about reducing the stigma surrounding mental health treatment and helping individuals feel heard, respected, and supported throughout their journey toward improved mental wellness.
